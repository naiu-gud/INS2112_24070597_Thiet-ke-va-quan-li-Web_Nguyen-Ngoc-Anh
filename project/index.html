Welcome

Small ideas can grow into beautiful things.

Greenfield Coding Club is a welcoming place for beginners and curious makers who want to learn how websites are built.

We practice step by step, share ideas, and help one another turn simple experiments into pages we are proud to show.

Discover our club

Our activities

What We Do

Our club activities are designed to make web design easier to understand and more enjoyable to practice.

  • Build small websites using HTML and CSS.
  • Practice page layouts and navigation.
  • Review one another's projects and share useful feedback.
  • Experiment with colors, spacing, images, and responsive design.

Grow with us

Why Join Us?

Friendly learning

Beginners can ask simple questions without feeling rushed or embarrassed.

Real practice

Every meeting includes something practical you can build, test, and improve.

Shared progress

You learn faster when people around you can explain ideas and celebrate small wins.

Summer workshop

Code, sunshine, and creative energy.

Our summer sessions mix focused coding practice with a relaxed atmosphere. Bring an idea, open your laptop, and spend time making something that feels like your own.
