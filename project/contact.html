Weekly Meetup

Where to find us

Greenfield Coding Club meets every Saturday afternoon at the Community Learning Room, 144 Xuan Thuy Street, Cau Giay District, Hanoi.

Our meetup space is relaxed and beginner-friendly. Bring a laptop if you have one, along with a question, an idea, or something you would like to build.

New Members Welcome

Your first visit can be simple.

You do not need previous coding experience to join. Come by, meet the community, and see how a small idea can grow into a real web project.
